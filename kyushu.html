Before we fly

    On the road

    • Volcanic sites are best before midday. Cloud builds over Aso in the afternoon.
    • Nakadake crater access changes with the volcanic alert level. Check the same morning before driving up.
    • Sakurajima ferry takes the car without a reservation. About ¥1,700 one way, paid on the Sakurajima side.
    • Shinkansen on 8 Dec: bags over 160 cm total (L+W+H) need an oversized-luggage seat reservation.
